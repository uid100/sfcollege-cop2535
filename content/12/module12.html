Wrap-up

Congratulations on completing the course! This module serves as a wrap-up to review the key concepts and techniques covered throughout the semester.

Modules in this course have covered a range of topics, including data structures, algorithms, and their applications. We have explored arrays, linked lists, stacks, queues, hash tables and graph data structures, along with their associated algorithms for searching, sorting, and traversal.

These data structures concepts have just been an introduction to computer science and data science. The expectation is that you will recognize these structures and algorithms and as you encounter and employ them, your understanding and knowledge will deepen.

Algorithm complexity and efficiency, as a function of data size over time will drive architectural decisions nad solution designs, and the choice of data structures and algorithms will impact the performance and scalability of applications and systems.
