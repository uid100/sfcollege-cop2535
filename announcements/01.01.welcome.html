So what can you expect?

There are 12 (13?) weeks in the term. We are using one of Tony Gaddis' stepping through... series of books, for the C++ language. Take a day or two now to refresh your knowledge of C++ or to pick up the syntax if it isn't familiar. We'll be starting in Chapter 2, but quickly jumping to more advanced concepts. You'll be using this book, and probably need to refer to others, and online for more background information, and trying out the concepts to explore more.

 

We will use the Canvas course as a guide through the subject material. This course is not new, and not new to me, but the presentation in Canvas is recently refreshed, please let me know if you see something that doesn't make sense, or doesn't look correct, and I'll do my best to clarify, or correct the information as quickly as I can. Thanks in advance for your patience and help!

 

Speaking of online, the college lists this as a web course. You'll also hear it referred to as asynchronous. We won't have scheduled meetings, but we do have a class schedule to accomplish. The Canvas course is designed to guide you through, but we'll do it together, and I will make an effort to not let you feel alone through the process.
